# Contribution Guide

English | [日本語](CONTRIBUTING.ja.md)

## About Pull Requests

When submitting a pull request (PR), please follow these guidelines:

- **Explain why the change is necessary in your own words.**
- Understand the changes you submit, and perform your own review and necessary testing.
- Comply with this project's license and contribution rules.

These conditions apply regardless of whether AI is used or not.

## Contributions Using AI

PRs created using AI are also welcome. We do not reject PRs simply because AI was used. Decisions are made based on the purpose, content, and quality of the changes.

For the policy on AI usage in the development of this project, please refer to the [AI Usage Policy](AI_POLICY.md).
